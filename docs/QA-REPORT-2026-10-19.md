## 4. Discovered Defects & Selector Anomalies
Fixed in this run (all re-verified):
- **D1 High – Teach could not capture Flow's prompt box.** ProseMirror never fires `click`; Teach now captures on `mousedown` (`content/calibrate.ts`).
- **D2 Critical – Video approval never clicked.** Flow's Approve is `div[role=radio][aria-label=Approve]`. Every video job failed as NO_OUTPUT after resubmitting the prompt 3 times. Added an exact `^approve$` radio strategy, and the adapter now scans every match, so a stale approval can't hide the current one (`shared/pack.ts`, `content/adapter.ts`).
- **D3 Critical – Live videos were never detected.** Finished videos render only as `img[alt="Generated video thumbnail"]`; the `<video>` (and its signed URL) mounts on hover. FlowForge now hovers new thumbnails and keys media by Flow's asset id (`content/media.ts`).
- **D4 High – Reference upload never attached.** Uploads land in the picker's Recent list and need to be selected, then "Add to prompt". Each retry also re-uploaded the file to the user's project (3 copies) (`content/flowui.ts`).
- **D5 High – Storyboard last-frame chaining broke on live Flow.** The video CDN is cross-origin for the content script, so frame extraction never ran. Added a background `fetchMedia` message, restricted to Flow media hosts (`background/index.ts`).
- **D6 Medium – A new queue wasn't selected**, so the next quick-add went to another queue (race in `sidepanel/App.tsx`).
- **D7 High – A parked video could be resubmitted.** Text like "scheduled… waiting in the queue due to high demand" now keeps waiting instead of becoming NO_OUTPUT.
- **D8 High – Wrong and duplicate outputs.** Flow's grid is virtualised and its image URLs rotate, so older tiles re-entered as "new" (an n:1 job got 3 files, a video job got 2 old images), and the chat copy of a tile was counted twice. Fixed with `data-media-id` keys, a newest-first grid anchor, src de-duplication, and "a video job only finishes on a video".
- **D8b High – 1:1 images saved at half resolution (512 px).** Flow URLs carry `=s512-rw`; FlowForge now fetches and downloads `=s0`, the original 1024 px, matching Flow's "1K Original size".
- **D9 Medium – A deleted workflow came back** (the unmount flush re-saved it).
- **D10 Medium – Import duplicate detection ignored `{{vars}}`**, dropping legitimate rows (regression test added).
- **D11 Low – Queue search `#10`** didn't match index 10.
- **D12 Low – Storyboard prompt** was missing the period after the shot type.
- **D13 High (privacy) – Diagnostics leaked prompts** through log lines ("Started #n: …").

Open (not fixed):
- **D14 Low – Status pill can show "Flow UI not recognised"** on a ready project until a probe or state refresh.
- **D15 Info – The license key in the test prompt fails signature checks** against `tools/license/private.key` (likely an older keypair).
- **D16 Low – Min delay above max delay is accepted in the UI** (the engine guards it).
- **D17 Low – No error text when the local agent is not running.**
- **D18 Low – Batch Delete of jobs has no confirmation.**
- **D19 Low – After a successful ⤓ re-download, the job keeps its old error message.**
- **D20 Low – An `@mention` that matches no Library asset gives no "Not in your Library" warning.**
- **D21 Low – "Use as reference" creates `job-1-1` with no alias.**
- **D22 Low – Inline `[aspect:]` tags in a workflow Prompt node are sent literally.**
- **D23 Low – NameModal doesn't close on Esc; the selected queue isn't remembered across panel reloads.**
- **D24 Low – The mock e2e "REST job" check fails intermittently** (agent pairing timing), 2 of 6 runs.
- **O1 Observation – For `[n: 2] [aspect: 1:1]`, Flow said "two images" but rendered one tile.** FlowForge saved 1 and warned "Asked for 2 outputs, got 1."
- **O2 Observation – Stop cancels Flow's agent response, but the image still appeared later**, and may still be billed.
- **O3 – Selector pack contents changed** (approve radio, `newestFirstItem`); bump the pack version before release.

## 5. Final Release Verdict
**NO-GO as of the start of this run, conditional GO with these fixes.** Before the fixes, every live video job, every reference upload and every storyboard chain failed, and image jobs could save the wrong or low-resolution files. All of these are fixed and re-verified live, the automated suites are green, and the anti-abuse paths behave safely. Remaining before release: bump the pack version, re-run one full live pass on a clean profile with IDM present, and decide on O2 (warn users that Stop may not refund).
